Typing

This is a bit of an unusual interest, you don't normally hear someone they type for "fun" or necessarily enjoy typing at all. But I started typing for fun in 2nd grade, when a friend/classmate introduced this car racing typing website, Nitro Type, to me. Since then, I've completed LOTS of races and slowly have gotten faster and faster at typing at upwards of 160 words per minute (WPM). I also type on Monkeytype for fun, it has a very clean and minimalistic UI that makes it enjoyable!

My profiles: Nitro Type, Monkeytype

Badminton 🏸

Badminton is very special to me because it's where I met 2 of my best friends freshman year 🥺. We made lots of memories together in the short season we had. Some of the most memorable were eating taco bell together on the bus to conference, practicing together in the fieldhouse every day, and awards night (one of the last times we were on the same team :( ). A funny story I have from sophomore year though, was that on the morning of one of the season's first invites, we found out that we were actively driving to the school for our game. That was one of the craziest feelings because I hadn't seen them in a while, and we were playing at the same invite just on different teams. It was quite bittersweet.
